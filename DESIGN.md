# LumoPay — DESIGN.md

Purpose: a design-fidelity study in the layout language of a large wallet homepage, rebranded as a fictional wallet. No login, no credential form, no real payment function.

Layout direction: Product in action. The hero shows the wallet working (balance, a send in progress, recent activity) in a phone built in CSS, next to a large navy headline on a pale blue field.

Reference structure (paraphrased, not copied): white sticky header, pale hero, category strip, pay / send / save tabbed section, dark security band, card section, business split section, final navy call to action, dense legal footer.

Brand:
- Name: LumoPay (fictional)
- Mark: two overlapping circles, pure graphic, no letters inside the glyph. Wordmark sits beside it as plain text.
- Navy ink: #001435
- Primary blue: #0070e0
- Deep blue: #003087
- Pale field: #eef4fb
- Page background: #ffffff sections alternating with #f5f7fa
- Type: "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif. Headlines bold, tight tracking, solid navy. No gradient text.

Signature scroll moment: as the visitor scrolls away from the hero, the phone lifts and tilts slightly and its activity list advances one row, scrubbed by scroll position. Static under prefers-reduced-motion.

Rules honoured: no login anywhere, no form collecting credentials, fake data only, fictional-brand disclaimer in the footer, touch targets 44px+, alt/aria labels, contrast checked.
